Marketing Performance Diagnostics

Marketing performance summary and a detailed funnel showing where customers convert, where they drop off, and how efficiently campaigns perform.

  • Marketing Performance Summary: YTD KPIs, Cross-Sell NAP and Retention SAP panels, monthly trends, and campaign performance vs forecast

Page 03

Payment Optimization

Operational drivers of retention, payment reliability, and online service, informed by the existing Retention Report.

What's inside
  • Business Outcomes: issues, buy rate, four-month persistency, and relationships saved, aligned to the Payment Optimization initiative
  • Payment Optimization: bills and payment failure rate
  • Online Service: users and self-service ratio
  • Comparisons: selected month, quarter, or year with Base / Stretch goals, status, and period-specific trends; relationships saved uses monthly Actual and goals through the selected month, while other metrics retain full-period goals
  • Visual comparisons: select a metric to explore its trend against Base and Stretch
